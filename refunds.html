Refund & Cancellation

Effective: September 22, 2026

Before payment

You may decline a quote at any time before payment and before translation work begins. There is no charge simply for requesting a quote unless a separate paid service is specifically agreed to in advance.

After payment but before work begins

If you cancel before translation work has started, the payment may generally be refunded, less any non-refundable transfer or transaction costs that actually apply.

After work has started

If translation work has already started, a full refund may not be available. Any refund will depend on the portion of work already completed and costs already incurred.

After delivery

Once the completed translation has been delivered, payments are generally non-refundable. If you believe there is a translation error, contact us promptly with the specific issue so it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, corrected.

Customer changes

If you change the source document, target language, formatting, deadline or scope after approving the quote, additional charges may apply. Significant changes may require a new quote.

Institution rejection

A refund is not automatically due because a third party, agency, school, employer, court or government office rejects a translation, particularly where special certification, notarization or formatting requirements were not disclosed and confirmed before the work began.

Duplicate or mistaken payments

If you believe you made a duplicate or incorrect Zelle payment, contact We Translate as soon as possible with the payment details so the issue can be reviewed.

How to request a cancellation or refund

Use the website contact/quote process and include the email used for the order, the document or request reference if available, and the reason for the request. A dedicated business email can be added here once confirmed.
