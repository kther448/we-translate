Privacy Policy

Effective: September 22, 2026
Translation requests can contain sensitive personal information. This policy explains the information involved in the service and how it should be handled.

1. Information you provide

When requesting a quote or translation, you may provide your email address, source and target languages, notes, and documents you choose to upload. Submitted documents may contain personal information belonging to you or other people.

2. How information is used

Information submitted through the website is used to review your request, prepare a quote, perform the translation, communicate with you, deliver completed work, process related customer service requests and maintain necessary business records.

3. Documents and sensitive information

Only submit documents that are necessary for the translation and that you have permission to share. Avoid including unrelated sensitive information when it is not needed for the service.

4. Payment information

Payments are currently made through Zelle. We Translate does not need your online banking password or login credentials and will never ask you to provide them through this website.

5. Service providers

Hosting, email, file-storage or form-processing providers may process information on our behalf when needed to operate the website and deliver translation services. The exact providers may change as the website's backend is finalized.

6. Retention

Documents and request information should be kept only as long as reasonably necessary to complete the translation, handle revisions or disputes, maintain required business records, and comply with legal obligations. Retention periods may vary depending on the request.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect submitted documents. No online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Sharing

We Translate does not sell customer documents or personal information. Information may be shared with service providers that help operate the service, when required by law, or when necessary to protect legal rights and prevent fraud or misuse.

9. Your choices

You may contact We Translate to ask questions about information associated with your request or to request deletion where legally and operationally possible.

10. Children

This service is not designed for children to submit documents independently. A parent, guardian or authorized adult should handle requests involving a minor's documents.

11. Policy updates

This policy may be updated as the website, hosting, upload system or business processes change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

12. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the website's quote/contact process. A dedicated privacy or business email can be added here once confirmed.
