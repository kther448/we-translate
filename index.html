FAQ

A few things to know before you send your document.

How long does a translation take?

Most standard requests are targeted for 24–48 hours after the quote and payment are confirmed. Larger or more complex files may take longer.

How much will it cost?

Most standard documents fall in the $25–$50 range. We confirm the exact quote after reviewing the file.

Which languages do you support?

English, Spanish, French and Haitian Creole.

Do you accept official documents?

Yes, you can submit official or unofficial documents for review. If a receiving institution requires a specific certification, notarization, translator credential or format, tell us before work begins so the requirement can be confirmed.

How do I pay?

Zelle is currently accepted. Wait until your final quote is confirmed before sending payment.
